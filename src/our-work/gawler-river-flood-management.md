### The brief

Economic assessment of flood mitigation options along the Gawler River in the north of Adelaide, South Australia. It was an assessment of additional intervention options to the existing flood retention dam. These include both structural and non-structural flood mitigation measures or different bundles of both.

### Our approach

It required the integration of flood modelling data with impact analysis for dwellings, businesses and farms across the region for the assessment period, under a climate change scenario.

### The outcome

The assessment demonstrated how the range of methods, tools, practices and capabilities used for assessing and evaluating flood risk reduction strategies and actions could be evolved and expanded in the face of climate change.
