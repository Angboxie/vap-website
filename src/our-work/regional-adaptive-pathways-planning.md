sections:
  - heading: Our approach
    body: The NSW Department of Planning & Environment (DPE), in collaboration with CSIRO and VAP, developed a systems- and values-based methodology for assessing multi-hazard disaster risks and identifying adaptation pathways for safe and affordable housing in the Illawarra Shoalhaven region. The project addressed how climate change, population growth, inequities, and net zero transitions interact to influence regional planning decisions, with a deliberate alignment to the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
  - heading: The outcome
    body: The work integrated hazard modelling, feasibility analysis, and extensive stakeholder engagement, including First Nations, community, government, and private sector participants, to co-design future housing and land use options that are resilient to multiple hazard events and are adaptable to changing circumstances in the future. The project demonstrated how participatory approaches and globally aligned evaluation frameworks can strengthen regional planning and build capability for adaptive, evidence-based decision-making under climate change.
